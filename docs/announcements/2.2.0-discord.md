# Discord announcement — InspiredLearning 2.2.0

Draft prepared after the successful production deployment and checks on 30 October 2026. Not posted or sent.

## Message

**InspiredLearning’s refreshed portal is live!**

I've given the portal a new look, with original cartoon illustrations, a clearer layout and smoother Material 3 motion that respects reduced-motion preferences.

**What’s changed**
- A new illustrated homepage and clearer learner/admin layouts.
- Better-aligned Settings and Sign out actions.
- Your Google profile photo now opens the account menu — no separate three-dot button.
- **14 fonts**, **6 palettes**, and light/dark/system appearance in **Settings**.
- A dedicated administrator portal for authorized staff.

Your existing Google account, credits and certificates carry over. Use the **same Google account you used for your workshop**.

**Open your portal:** https://inspi.in
**For authorized staff:** https://admin.inspi.in

For a record correction, go to **Profile → Record support**. If an older downloaded certificate has no signature, download a fresh copy from the portal.

Try it out and share any layout or usability problems here so I can improve them.
